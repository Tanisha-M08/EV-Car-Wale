CHARGING

Charging

Charging time depends on the battery size and charger type. A standard home AC charger (3.3 kW) can take 8-12 hours for a full charge, while DC fast chargers (50 kW+) can charge from 10-80% in 40-60 minutes. Use our Charging Time Calculator for precise estimates.
Yes, most EV owners charge at home using a standard 15A socket or a dedicated AC wall box. Our Apartment Charging Guide provides detailed information about setting up charging in flats and gated communities.
Use our Charging Stations map to find nearby charging points. Filter by connector type (CCS2, CHAdeMO, Type 2), charging speed, and availability. The map covers thousands of public charging stations across India.
BATTERY

Battery & Warranty

Most EV batteries are designed to last 8-10 years or 1,50,000-2,00,000 km, whichever comes first. Actual lifespan depends on charging habits, climate, and usage patterns. Manufacturers typically offer 8-year / 1,60,000 km battery warranties.
Battery warranties typically cover manufacturing defects and capacity degradation below 70% of original capacity. Most warranties include free replacement if the battery fails within the warranty period. Check with the manufacturer for specific terms.
Keep battery charge between 20-80% for daily use, avoid frequent DC fast charging, park in shade during Indian summers, and avoid letting the battery discharge to 0% regularly. Our Battery Health Guide has detailed maintenance tips.
