OVERVIEW

Company Overview

EV Car Wale is India's fastest-growing electric vehicle discovery platform. Founded with a vision to accelerate the transition to sustainable mobility, we provide comprehensive, unbiased information about every electric vehicle available in India. From detailed specifications and real-world range estimates to pricing, comparisons, and charging infrastructure, EV Car Wale is the definitive resource for anyone considering an electric vehicle.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

EV Car Wale was born from a simple observation: buying an electric vehicle in India was unnecessarily difficult. Information was scattered, specifications were buried in PDFs, and honest comparisons were nearly impossible.

What started as a side project — a simple spreadsheet comparing a handful of EVs — quickly grew into something much larger. Our founders, passionate about both automobiles and sustainability, realised that the biggest barrier to EV adoption wasn't the technology — it was the lack of a single, trusted place to research and compare.

Today, EV Car Wale serves millions of visitors every month, helping them navigate the exciting world of electric mobility. We've grown from a simple comparison tool to a full-fledged platform featuring detailed reviews, charging station maps, interactive calculators, expert insights, and an AI assistant available 24/7.

But our mission remains the same: to make EV adoption in India simple, transparent, and accessible for everyone.
