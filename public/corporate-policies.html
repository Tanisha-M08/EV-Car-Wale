POLICIES

Corporate Policies

How we operate with integrity, responsibility, and purpose.

ETHICS

Code of Ethics

EV Car Wale is committed to the highest ethical standards in all our operations. We believe that trust is earned through transparency, honesty, and integrity.

  • Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency in every interaction with users, partners, and stakeholders.
  • Objectivity: Our content and recommendations are unbiased and data-driven, free from external influence.
  • Confidentiality: We protect user data and respect privacy at all times.
  • Professionalism: We maintain the highest standards of competence and conduct in our work.
COMPLIANCE

Regulatory Compliance

EV Car Wale complies with all applicable laws and regulations in India, including:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and related data protection regulations.
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • Copyright and intellectual property laws.
  • Advertising and marketing regulations.

We regularly review our practices to ensure ongoing compliance with evolving legal requirements.

SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainability Commitment

As a platform dedicated to electric mobility, sustainability is at the core of everything we do. Our commitments include:

  • Promoting accurate information about EV environmental benefits to accelerate adoption.
  • Minimising our own digital carbon footprint through efficient web practices.
  • Partnering with organisations that share our sustainability values.
  • Regularly publishing insights and data that support India's net-zero goals.
RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS

Responsible Business Practices

We believe in doing business responsibly. Our practices include:

  • Data Responsibility: We handle user data with care, following privacy-by-design principles.
  • Content Accuracy: We verify information before publishing and correct errors promptly.
  • Accessibility: We strive to make our platform accessible to all users, regardless of ability.
  • Fair Partnerships: We engage with partners and advertisers transparently, clearly distinguishing editorial content from sponsored material.
CUSTOMER COMMITMENT

Customer Commitment

Our users are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to:

  • Providing accurate, unbiased, and up-to-date information.
  • Responding to feedback and continuously improving the platform.
  • Offering free access to all core features and tools.
  • Being transparent about how we operate and make decisions.
