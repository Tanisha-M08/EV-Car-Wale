SECTION 1 // NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE SURVEY

The Current State of EV Charging Across India

Electric vehicle adoption in India has reached an inflection point. Driven by favorable government policies, reduced battery costs, and expanding charging networks, EV sales crossed 5,00,000 units annually in 2026. However, the speed of adoption relies heavily on charging availability.

Today, over 65% of EV charging occurs at home or workplace parking basements via Level 2 AC chargers. Meanwhile, highway charging infrastructure is scaling rapidly to unlock intercity EV travel across key transport routes.

Category Charger Type Power Output Primary Use Case
Level 1 AC Standard 15A Socket 3.3 kW Overnight home charging (10-14 hours)
Level 2 AC Single / Three Phase Wallbox 7.2 kW – 22 kW Homes, apartments, workplaces (5-7 hours)
Level 3 DC Fast CCS2 Dual Gun Station 30 kW – 120 kW Highways & commercial hubs (30-45 mins)
Level 3 Ultra-Fast 800V High Voltage DC 150 kW – 350 kW Premium highway hubs & bus depots (15-20 mins)
SECTION 2 // HIGHWAY CORRIDORS & URBAN DENSITY

Highway Electrification & Apartment Complex Charging

Highways connecting major economic hubs—such as the Delhi-Jaipur Highway, Mumbai-Pune Expressway, Bengaluru-Chennai Highway, and Hyderabad-Vijayawada Corridor—now feature dual-gun DC fast chargers placed every 30 to 45 kilometers.

URBAN CHARGING DENSITY

Tier 1 cities average 20-30 public charging locations per 100 sq km. Malls, office parks, and hotel parking lots lead urban charging accessibility.

APARTMENT RWA BYLAWS

Model building bylaws in 18 states now mandate 20% EV-ready parking spots in all new residential and commercial construction.

SECTION 3 // MYTHS VS FACTS

Common Myths About EV Charging Infrastructure

MYTH #1

"There are no charging stations outside major metro cities."

FACT: Over 3,800 DC fast chargers are operational on state and national highways, enabling long-distance road trips across major Indian states.

MYTH #2

"Charging an EV takes hours even at fast chargers."

FACT: Modern 60 kW – 120 kW DC fast chargers replenish an EV battery from 10% to 80% in 30 to 45 minutes.
