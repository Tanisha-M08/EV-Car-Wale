Financial Economics of Electric Ownership

  • ✓ 85% Fuel Cost Reduction: Driving 15,000 km per year in an EV costs approximately ₹16,500 in electricity compared to ₹1,27,500 for petrol.
  • ✓ Lower Maintenance Expense: EVs have 80% fewer moving parts (no engine oil, spark plugs, clutch plates, or exhaust systems), saving ₹30,000+ over 5 years.
  • ✓ Break-Even Timeline: Higher upfront EV purchase prices are fully recovered within 3 to 4 years of typical driving.
