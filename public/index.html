BATTERY ENGINEERING

How to Maximize Battery Life

Follow these simple charging and maintenance practices to preserve cell integrity and extend lifecycle.

1
Limit DC Fast Charging

Avoid daily DC fast charging. High-power currents generate rapid heat, accelerating cell degradation over time.

2
Maintain 20–80% Charge

Keep battery charge between 20% and 80% for daily commuting. This avoids extreme voltage stress on cell structures.

3
Avoid Extreme Heat

Park in shaded spaces or garages during peak summer. High ambient heat speeds up passive electrochemical loss.

4
Use Scheduled Charging

Program off-peak charging schedules so the battery completes charging just before departure, avoiding prolonged 100% states.

5
Update BMS Software

Ensure firmware updates are installed. Manufacturers regularly refine battery management system parameters for efficiency.

6
Monitor Tyre Pressure

Keep tyres properly inflated. Incorrect pressure increases rolling friction, forcing higher battery draw and reducing range.

THE FUTURE IS NOW

Why Choose an Electric Vehicle?

Discover the key financial, environmental, and performance advantages of moving to clean energy.

EVs cost significantly less per kilometer compared to petrol or diesel cars. Charging at home is highly economical, saving you thousands in monthly fuel bills and utility charges.

Electric motors deliver instant torque, giving you quick and silent acceleration. The floor-mounted battery pack also provides a low center of gravity for superior high-speed stability and handling.

With zero tailpipe emissions, EVs significantly reduce air pollution and greenhouse gases, helping create a cleaner, healthier, and quieter environment for future generations.

Wake up to a fully charged car every single day by plugging it in overnight at home. Public fast charging networks are also expanding rapidly for quick highway top-ups.

With fewer moving parts (no engine block, pistons, gearbox, spark plugs, or catalytic converter), EVs require far less maintenance and have lower servicing costs than traditional petrol cars.

Take advantage of central and state-level EV incentives, including direct purchase subsidies, road tax exemptions, and registration fee waivers across major Indian states.

OBJECTIVE PURCHASE ASSESSMENT

Should You Buy an Electric Vehicle?

An honest, detailed analysis comparing the practical day-to-day advantages of ownership with critical parameters to consider.

THE ADVANTAGES
✓
Zero Tailpipe Emissions

Immediate reduction of urban carbon footprint and zero exhaust pollutants.

✓
Lower Fuel Cost

Electricity running costs are roughly 1/5th the price of petrol per kilometer.

✓
Lower Maintenance

Fewer mechanical components translate to lower service costs and diagnostic times.

✓
Instant Torque

Smooth, seamless acceleration from a standstill with zero gear shifts or engine lag.

✓
Silent Driving

Minimal cabin noise and vibration levels, creating a highly relaxed driving environment.

✓
Tax Benefits

Enjoy direct income tax deductions on EV loans under Section 80EEB rules.

THE CONSIDERATIONS
✦
Charging Availability

Public highway fast charging networks are still expanding and require trip planning.

✦
Initial Purchase Cost

Higher upfront purchase cost than equivalent petrol cars due to battery pack costs.

✦
Long Highway Trips

Adds charging stop durations to your total travel time (typically 30–50 min per stop).

✦
Apartment Charging Setup

Installing a charger in multi-owner apartments requires approvals and wiring clearance.

✦
Range in Weather

Heavy cabin heating or air conditioning can decrease real-world range by 10-15%.

✦
Battery Replacement Cost

Though batteries outlast standard ownership cycles, replacement is expensive post-warranty.
