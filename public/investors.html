OVERVIEW

Company Overview

EV Car Wale is India's premier electric vehicle discovery platform, connecting millions of consumers with the information they need to make confident EV purchasing decisions. Our platform features comprehensive vehicle databases, comparison tools, charging station locators, interactive calculators, and AI-powered assistance — all designed to accelerate EV adoption in India.

Investor Information

As India rapidly transitions to electric mobility, EV Car Wale is uniquely positioned to capture the growing demand for EV information and discovery tools.
